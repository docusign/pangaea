# Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

This policy applies to the Docusign Pangaea Chrome DevTools extension.

The extension does not collect, transmit to external servers, or sell user data. It does not use
analytics or tracking services.

When you run an audit, the extension accesses the inspected page's URL, text, structure, and layout
to identify internationalization issues. This processing takes place locally in your browser. Page
content and audit results are not sent to Docusign or third-party servers.

You can print audit reports or save them as PDFs using the browser's print dialog. Reports may
contain page URLs and content from the inspected page. You control where these reports are saved and
whether they are shared.

For privacy questions about the extension, open an issue in the
[project repository](https://github.com/docusign/pangaea/issues). Do not include personal
information, confidential page content, or sensitive audit results in public issues.
